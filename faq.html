Absolutely not! Jumbo Ventures welcomes all Tufts students — aspiring founders, investors, designers, engineers, or anyone simply curious about startups and venture capital. Who knows, you might meet your future co-founder or join your first startup through our community.

No, we don’t directly invest in startups. Jumbo Ventures is a student-run organization focused on education, networking, and community. We make introductions, host pitch events, and connect student founders with mentors, alumni, and potential investors. Tufts students can also access funding through the Derby Entrepreneurship Center (competitions and grants) — those aren’t affiliated with or managed by Jumbo Ventures, but we’re happy to guide you toward them.

We’re excited to have you! Browse our semester-long programs, upcoming events, workshops, and recruitment opportunities. Follow us on LinkedIn and Instagram (@tuftsjumboventures) to stay in the loop.

Jumbo Ventures primarily serves Tufts undergraduates, but we’re always open to collaborating with graduate students who are passionate about innovation and startups. If you’re interested in partnering, mentoring, or joining our programs, reach out to one of our board members directly.
